Conditional scalar-curvature map (analytic reasoning; no Lean proof claimed)

1. Frozen input: EFMW-LAGRANGIAN-C1, scalar-curvature subsector, gauge fields zero.
   Signature (-+++), reduced Planck units Mpl=c=hbar=1. Write dimensionless scalar
   coordinates x_a=phi_a/Mpl; coefficients below are expressed in these units.
   The coefficient of R is F(x)/2, with

   F = 1 - sum_a xi_a x_a^2 - 2 sum_a g_Ea x_a
       - 2 sum_(a<b) h_Eab x_a x_b.

   Define q_a = dF/dx_a at a constant background x0:
   q_a = -2 xi_a x0_a - 2 g_Ea - 2 sum_(b != a) h_Eab x0_b.
   Terms involving gauge invariants are absent only because gauge backgrounds
   and gauge perturbations are restricted in this calculation.

2. Assume F0>0. For g_Einstein = F g_Jordan, the scalar kinetic matrix is
   K_ab = delta_ab/F + (3/(2 F^2)) q_a q_b.
   This is a local field-space metric. It need not admit a global simultaneous
   canonical redefinition for all fields. We diagonalize only at the background.
   Known framework: D. I. Kaiser, Conformal Transformations with Multiple Scalar
   Fields, Phys. Rev. D 81, 084044 (2010), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.81.084044,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.1159 . No novelty is claimed for this transformation.

3. Add ordinary matter minimally coupled to the Jordan metric as an EXPLICIT
   extension of source-free C1. Its Einstein-frame conformal factor is A=F^(-1/2).
   Define the dimensionless local coupling vector with the canonical kinetic
   normalization -(1/2) delta_ij dchi_i dchi_j:

   alpha = -(1/(2F)) K^(-1/2) q.
   alpha^2 = q^T K^(-1) q / (4F^2) = |q|^2 / (4F + 6 |q|^2).

   The last equality follows from the rank-one inverse formula (or decomposing
   parallel and perpendicular to q). Alpha here has this explicit convention;
   factors of sqrt(2) differ in some scalar-tensor conventions. No external bound
   is transplanted without matching conventions and compact-object charges.
   Numerical check: 1000 samples; independently invert K and compare closed form.
   A second check differentiates F by central differences and compares q.

4. On a locally Minkowski, constant-scalar, zero-gauge background satisfying the
   background equations, the linear Jordan metric perturbation has conformal part
   delta_gJ = -(delta_F/F0) eta. For a massless scalar plane wave, its transverse
   breathing strain is h_b = -delta_F/F0 = -q dot delta_x/F0.
   The scalar stress from a minimally coupled canonical perturbation about a
   stationary constant background begins at quadratic order. Thus q=0 gives no
   linear conformal breathing signal in this restricted limit, even when scalar
   degrees of freedom propagate. This is not a statement about rolling backgrounds,
   nonlinear scalarization, nonzero gauge backgrounds or massive longitudinal response.
   Require potential stationarity, compatible vacuum energy, stable mass eigenvalues
   and validity of the effective description before applying the local calculation.

5. A physical comparison still needs delta_x(f) generated by a compact binary,
   its scalar charges, propagation and detector response. In the massless constant-
   ratio case the catalog coefficient would be
   C_b(f) = h_b(f)/h_plus(f) = -q dot delta_x(f)/(F0 h_plus(f)).
   The released TS evidence integrates an assumed prior over phenomenological C_b;
   it is not a posterior for q, alpha, or any individual EFMW coefficient.
   No finite EFMW bound can be recovered from the aggregate evidence numbers alone.

Synthetic detector test (independent of the proposed EFMW source dynamics)

For each ideal Michelson, D=(u u^T-v v^T)/2. Contract D with polarization tensors.
For transverse orthonormal m,l and propagation direction n:
e_plus=mm-ll, e_cross=ml+lm, e_b=mm+ll, e_L=sqrt(2)nn.
Because trace(D)=0, F_L=-sqrt(2) F_b: breathing and longitudinal responses are
degenerate in this long-wavelength approximation.

With whitened tensor response matrix FT, define P=I-FT pinv(FT).
If P b != 0, let q_null=P b/|P b|. In a known unit-normalized temporal template,
z=q_null^T d is N(rho_null,1). Tensor amplitudes are projected out. Detection
threshold |z|>Phi^-1(0.995) yields theoretical 1% two-sided per-template FAP.
Analytic power = 1-Phi(threshold-rho)+Phi(-threshold-rho).
No unknown-sky, template-bank or time-window trials are included.

With two independent detector channels and rank(FT)=2, P is zero. An unrestricted
two-tensor waveform leaves no model-independent null channel in that network.
Additional waveform assumptions can still support comparisons; those assumptions
must be stated. The simulation does not reproduce the LVK effective-mode sampler.
